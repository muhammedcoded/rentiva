# Rentiva demo listing reference notes

These eight records are explicitly flagged `is_demo = true` and are not real Rentiva inventory or verified properties. Their photographs are illustrative references from Nigerian property/development websites. Confirm image-use permissions or replace them with Rentiva-owned/licensed images before public commercial launch.

Asking prices are calibrated against public asking-price snapshots available on 26 November 2026. Asking prices are not completed transaction prices or professional valuations.
